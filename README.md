# Battleship

A modern, responsive web-based **Battleship game** built with **JavaScript**, **HTML**, and **CSS**. Playable on both desktop and mobile, with drag-and-drop ship placement, touch support, and dynamic visual feedback.

## DEMO
https://joshua-2333.github.io/Battleship-/

---
##  Features

- **Classic Battleship gameplay** with 10x10 grids for player and enemy.  
- **Drag-and-drop ship placement** for desktop, tap-to-place for mobile.  
- **Touch and mobile-friendly** interface with responsive scaling.  
- **Ghost ship preview** for valid/invalid placements.  
- **Dynamic orientation toggle** with a button or `R` key (horizontal/vertical).  
- **Visual feedback**:
  - Hits turn cells red.  
  - Misses turn cells white.  
  - Sunk ships are highlighted in dark red.  
- **Messages overlay** showing actions like ship pick-up, invalid placement, and placement confirmation.  
- **Fully responsive layout** for desktops, tablets, and mobile devices.  
- **Customizable ships and grid size**.

---

## How to Play

- **Setup Phase:**

 - Drag ships from the sidebar onto your grid (desktop).

 - Tap ships and then tap the grid to place (mobile).

 - Press the Rotate button or R key to switch orientation.

 - Ensure ships do not overlap or go out of bounds; invalid placements are highlighted.

- **Battle Phase:**

 - Click or tap on enemy grid cells to attack.

 - Hits, misses, and sunk ships are visually updated.

 - Game ends when all ships of either player or enemy are sunk.
